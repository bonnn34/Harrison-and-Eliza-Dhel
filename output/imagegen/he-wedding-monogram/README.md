## Final prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: luxurious wedding monogram for invitations, wax seals, and stationery.
Primary request: Create one elegant, distinctive, timeless 2D wedding monogram featuring ONLY the clearly readable uppercase initials H and E.
Typography: custom refined high-contrast Roman serif lettering, graceful fine hairlines and sculpted thick strokes, subtle calligraphic terminals. Both letters equally prominent and unmistakably recognizable as H and E. Arrange H slightly left and E slightly right with a restrained graceful overlap, retaining all distinguishing strokes of each letter and balanced spacing.
Composition: centered compact harmonious emblem, balanced proportions, generous transparent margins, suitable for a wax seal and stationery. The initials occupy about 80 percent of the mark's visual emphasis.
Decorations: only two tiny airy botanical sprigs with a few delicate leaves, positioned as a restrained asymmetrical accent at the lower left and upper right, plus one or two understated fine flourishes that harmonize with the serif forms. Keep it minimal and clean; no full wreath, no frame, no flowers crowding the letters.
Color palette: one flat elegant muted dusty blue ink, approximately #52769A, consistent with a romantic pastel garden wedding. No other colors.
Style: polished flat 2D logo artwork with crisp edges and clean continuous lines; luxurious, romantic and restrained.
Text verbatim: H E. No ampersand, names, dates, taglines, labels, or other letters.
Background: fully transparent alpha, including all counters, gaps, spaces between leaves and outside the emblem. No paper texture, no white canvas, no checkerboard pattern baked into pixels.
Avoid: 3D effects, gradients, metallic effects, embossing, shadows, mockup surfaces, excessive ornamental details, illegible intertwined lettering.
Output: one high-resolution PNG, square canvas, ideally 2048 x 2048 pixels or larger, with true fully transparent background.
